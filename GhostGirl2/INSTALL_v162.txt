GHOST GIRL v1.62 — MYTHIC PLAYTEST & WAND SPRITES
Applies over v1.61. Small incremental overlay.

1. Back up GhostGirl2 and saved games, then close Godot.
2. Extract the ZIP into the project folder containing project.godot.
3. Overwrite matching files and reopen Godot to import the new wand textures.

NEW DEBUG TEST BUTTON:
- Select any equippable item in the backpack during a Godot debug play session.
- The detail pane shows PLAYTEST · MYTHIC. Click it to preview signature #4.
- Click CLEAR MYTHIC TEST to restore its natural appearance.
- Preview also works for dresses. Equip the item to see its glow.
- This is an in-memory override only: it does not modify rare_visual_mod,
  cosmetic_uid, stats, loot odds, saved favorites, or save data.
- The button is hidden and overrides ignored in non-debug builds.
- Normal 1/65536 mythic odds are unchanged.

WAND SPRITES (FIRST PASS):
- The original crystal rose wand is retained.
- Moonlit Censer and Thorn Crescent add two new hand-held textured silhouettes.
- The player's saved cosmetic look deterministically picks one of the three.
- Each shares v1.61's animated Ghost-local parent and has a matching glow axis.
- Static isolated sprites are used with facing rotations; these are not yet
  individually frame-authored 8-frame attack/idle atlases.
- Ghost Girl's original character sprites and animation atlas are unchanged.
