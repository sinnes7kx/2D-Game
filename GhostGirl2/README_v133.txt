Ghost Girl v1.33 — Bellglass Echoes (incremental patch for v1.32)

1. Back up your v1.32 folder.
2. Extract the ZIP at the project root (the folder containing project.godot).
3. Overwrite matching files.
4. Launch with F5 in Godot. Defeat the Moor Warden before attempting the Bellglass Echoes.

New post-boss encounters in Hollow Veil Moor:
- Western Thread: target with F (rose-hook)
- Drowned Frost: target with Z (learned Moonfall, with a wand)
- Skyward Note: use C to jump, then a charged bow shot (right click)

Collect all three to unlock the unique Bellglass Reliquary accessory at the vault near the Warden arena. Saved using existing discovery flags; no save conversion.

Also fixes boss-summoned Hush Moths lingering after victory.
Validation: v1.32 and v1.33 offline tests pass. Godot engine playtest still required.
