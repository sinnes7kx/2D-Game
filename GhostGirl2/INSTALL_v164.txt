Ghost Girl v1.64 — Rare Item Fireflies
Apply over v1.63.

Adds subtle firefly-like ambient particles for equipped rare/cosmetic-seeded items.
- weapons: tiny motes around the glow span
- dress/accessories: soft drifting motes near the body and equipped accessories
- visual only, inspired by the environment fireflies
- no stat or save changes

Files changed:
- scripts/equipment_visuals.gd
- scripts/weapon_prestige_fx.gd

Install:
1. Close Godot.
2. Extract into the GhostGirl2 project folder.
3. Overwrite matching files.
4. Reopen Godot and playtest.
