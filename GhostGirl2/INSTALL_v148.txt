GARDEN BRIDGE: Find the small rose-route altar near the east-side entrance. Press E to begin. Hook the three numbered lanterns in order with F, crossing toward the old western rose anchors. A one-time chest appears on the far bank; return if inventory is full. The run is optional and replayable for a faster time with no extra drops. Wrong lantern costs four seconds; menu pauses the timer.

ECHO TRIALS: Opening inventory pauses countdown/timer (enemies were already paused); failed encounters clean up their wave and allow a fresh restart. Hooking a rose bell during an enemy attack windup returns 9 stamina.
