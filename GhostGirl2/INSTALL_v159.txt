GHOST GIRL v1.59 — FEINTS, WEAVING HEXES & MOONFALL COSMETICS
Incremental source patch for the installed v1.58 project.

INSTALL
1. Back up the whole GhostGirl2 project and your save files.
2. Close Godot and extract the ZIP into GhostGirl2 beside project.godot.
3. Merge the scripts directory; replace the seven matching .gd files.
4. Reopen Godot and let it parse/reimport. No sprites, scenes, save data
   or combat stats are overwritten.
5. Optional: run tests/validate_v159.py from the project root with Python 3.

GAMEPLAY
- Blade hunters occasionally feint after two normal windups, sidestepping
  with a distinct pale broken tell. They then perform a genuine telegraphed
  attack. The feint itself has no hitbox, damage, or dodge-counter reward.
- Occultists periodically release a single shallow weaving hex bolt.
  The alternate shot has a cool tracer; it retains original damage and
  swept player/wall raycast. It does not fire extra projectiles.
- These mechanics apply to existing encounters and the earlier combat trials.

COSMETICS
- New super-rare Moonfall Halo signature: approximately 1 in 131,072
  independent UID looks. The older Mythic Radiance remains approx. 1/65,536.
  Appearance UID is cryptographic 128-bit RNG; no central uniqueness registry.
- Weapons with Moonfall Halo get dual luminous rails and color-shifting motes.
- Dresses gain a compact lunar aura; rare wing/trinket looks shimmer.
- Saved looks keep their signatures. Mirrored item icons mark signature
  levels 4 and 5. The ALT item tooltip names them correctly.
- Saved favorite gradients now preview the saved UID rather than the
  currently selected look; clothing aura positioning is corrected.

SAFETY & SCOPE
Visual effects affect no damage, loot, hitbox, RNG for stats, or equipment slots.
Save schema is unchanged. Legacy numeric cosmetics and saved looks still load.
No original sprite images or art atlases were touched.

KNOWN TEST LIMITATION
The available environment does not have a Godot executable or a fully
playtested runtime build. Source/static/regression checks do NOT demonstrate
actual F5 gameplay. Use TEST_v159.txt for in-engine manual verification.
