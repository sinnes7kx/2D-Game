# Ghost Girl — collecting-first design direction

Approved 2026-11-24. Ghost Girl is a gothic weapon-collecting RPG with lightweight combat and atmospheric exploration. The primary reward is discovering and preserving extraordinarily individual weapons, not grinding for damage.

## Principles
- Cosmetic rarity is independent of combat quality and statistics. A normal sword can be a treasured anomaly.
- Each weapon's persistent cosmetic identity independently determines material, color, finish, glow, particles, ornament, shape, handle and later attack/impact presentation. Never use combat RNG or alter damage to generate appearance.
- Preserve approved Ghost Girl character art, existing save compatibility, and the gothic painterly aesthetic. Keep technical seed identifiers hidden in ordinary tooltips. Reproducible looks, favorites and protection matter.
- Make ordinary fights short and satisfying; reserve demanding combat for optional bosses. Few meaningful drops rather than inventory spam.
- Sanctum becomes a permanent collection vault and exhibition: favorites, locking, expanded storage, display pedestals and actual rolled visuals.
- Future inspection mode: enlarged animated appearance, rotate/preview attacks and toggle glow. Copy appearance code optionally, not in normal tooltip.
- Region-exclusive visual traits: Moonwake spectral steel; Moor mist; Ashglass molten glass; Briar living thorns; Observatory galaxies; Lily Pool iridescence.
- Rare discoveries get distinctive restrained audiovisual feedback; no paid random rolls or requirement to farm perfect stats.

## Roadmap
v1.77 independent cosmetic trait rolls and rarity foundation; v1.78 modular physical blade shapes/materials/handles/ornaments; v1.79 animated inspection; v1.80 vault/favorites/locking; v1.81 personal exhibition; v1.82 region-exclusive traits; v1.83 lightweight combat and loot progression; v1.84 anomaly discovery presentation.

Material rarity target (per eligible weapon): 60% standard, 25% unusual, 10% enchanted, 4% animated, 0.9% celestial, 0.09% ethereal, 0.01% anomaly. Tune through gameplay testing, independently of quality. Cosmetic identities are reproducible, not a guarantee of global uniqueness. All appearance changes must be visual only.
