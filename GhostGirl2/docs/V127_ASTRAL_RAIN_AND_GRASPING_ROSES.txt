GHOST GIRL v1.27 — ASTRAL RAIN & GRASPING ROSES
==============================================
Requires: v1.26 (Thornrose & Moonveil Bows).

NEW SPELLS
----------
Learn at two new relic altars near the starting room in Moonwake Crypt:
- Astral Rain Reliquary (near x=980, y=1060): press E to learn permanently.
  Equip a wand and press V to cast at the mouse cursor.
  40 stamina, 7.8-second cooldown, ~3.45-second area storm.
  Repeated, timed damage pulses. Decorative star particles never deal extra hits.
- Grasping Roses Reliquary (near x=1400, y=1060): press E to learn.
  Equip a wand and press T to cast.
  32 stamina, 7.1-second cooldown, ~3.1-second thorn-snare field.
  Each enemy can take one hit per cast; slow refreshes while inside.
  Elites keep their existing slow resistance.

Both spell effects inherit your equipped wand's two visual colors and rarity.
More dramatic ornaments are cosmetic and reserved for rare, set or unique wands.
Both spells respect stone walls and cannot be cast through solid cover.
Unlocks use the existing collected_pickups save dictionary; old saves remain valid.

BOW POLISH
----------
- Distinct release flare, brighter for charged shots.
- Four rare-only cosmetic arrow trails: Rose Comet, Crescent Halo,
  Frost Star Needles, Dusk Helix.
- Arrow collisions, normal single-hit and charged two-target pierce remain unchanged.
- No new weapon slots, damage projectiles or save fields.

INSTALL
-------
1. Back up your existing v1.26 Godot project.
2. Extract this patch directly into the folder with project.godot.
3. Overwrite matching files; open the game in Godot 4.7.
4. Study both reliquaries in Moonwake Crypt; equip a wand and try V and T.
5. Equip Thornrose or Moonveil to test bow release flares and rare arrows.
