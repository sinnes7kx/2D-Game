GHOST GIRL v1.68 — Polished Mythic Layers & Larger Inventory
INCREMENTAL PATCH — APPLY OVER v1.67

INSTALL
1. Close Godot and back up both the GhostGirl2 project folder and your
   existing user://ghost_girl_treasures_v2.json save first.
2. Extract this patch directly into the GhostGirl2 folder containing project.godot.
   Overwrite matching scripts and scenes.
3. Reopen the project, wait for Godot's imports, then playtest.

UPDATES
• Dedicated bow locomotion sprites are selected instead of idle/walk body
  sprites that contain the painted rose hook. All original bow attacks remain.
• The painted bow is visible during movement, without a detached carry shard.
• Independent full-spectrum bow-limb and bowstring gradients from the SAVED
  128-bit cosmetic UID. No cosmetic IDs displayed to players.
• Mythic effects appear as brief seeded bursts; soft taper replaces blunt sword
  edges. Rare effects do not cause any extra hit or change damage.
• Mythic wand bolts, beams and lances get a glowing moontear core and one of
  four seeded animated accents; original collision and damage are unchanged.
• Backpack grid expands from 10x4 to 10x6 per page, eight pages, capacity 72.
  Old 4-row grid positions are migrated by preserving item page and local row;
  the save adds a small 'grid_rows' metadata key. Items, indices, affixes, and
  equipment IDs are preserved. A backup is required before downgrading.
• The extra explanatory lines are removed from the inventory panel. The
  selected/hovered item stays compact; hold ALT for rare/Mythic effects,
  flavor text, spell forms and the bow's two independently rolled gradients.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
• This patch is source-validated only: no live Godot runtime/F5 test was
  available here. Inspect all 4 bow directions, Mythic display, projectile
  trails, and save/load migration inside Godot.
• The original bow full-body idle/walk art is used rather than introducing
  unrelated/redrawn character frames. Fine pose refinement remains possible.
• No new damage, affixes, item generation probabilities or gameplay collision
  behavior was intentionally changed.
