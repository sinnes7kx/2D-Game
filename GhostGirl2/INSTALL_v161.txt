Ghost Girl v1.61 — Wand Synchronization & Combat Variety
====================================================
Apply this incremental overlay ON TOP OF v1.60.

Installation
1. Back up the project (and your game save).
2. Close the Godot editor.
3. Extract this ZIP into the GhostGirl2 folder that contains project.godot.
4. Overwrite matching files, reopen Godot, and allow the asset import to finish.
5. Run the project using F5.

What changes
- The held-wand Sprite2D now sits under Ghost, the same AnimatedSprite2D
  parent as the glow renderer. Its pose is controlled in Ghost-local pixels.
  The gameplay Hand marker and rose-hook launch location are unchanged.
- Wand glow follows the actual hand-held artwork transform (with a fallback
  if no texture has loaded), and casting softly brightens the crystal head.
- Non-elite blade hunters sometimes circle around Ghost Girl instead of
  charging directly. Their sidestep cannot deal damage.
- Blade-hunter missed lunges have a brief visibly exposed recovery; the
  existing recovery-hit bonus is not increased.
- Occultists drift sideways during their existing post-cast recovery,
  using collision raycasts rather than passing through walls.

Changed files
- scenes/player.tscn
- scripts/equipment_visuals.gd
- scripts/weapon_prestige_fx.gd
- scripts/gothic_hunter.gd

Compatibility
- No save format, loot RNG, item stats, spell damage, enemy strike damage,
  enemy hitbox, or character artwork changes.
- The wand art from v1.60 is reused. Do not install on v1.59 directly.
- v1.59 feints/weaving bolts and previous cosmetic effects are retained.

Manual playtest checklist
- Idle/walk/cast in all four directions with a wand: overlay and glow stay
  attached throughout animation and returning to idle.
- Cast with and without a sword in inventory; the equipped-slot behavior
  remains unchanged.
- Hook targets still fire from Hand and do not shift with wand animation.
- Fight non-elite blade hunters: observe occasional short circle approach,
  then ordinary telegraphed attacks. Dodge a lunge and punish its miss.
- Fight an occultist close to and away from walls: it should slide slightly
  after firing but not pass through walls or fire more projectiles.
- Confirm save/reload, inventory, rare item icon flipping, and cosmetic
  gradient favorites still behave like v1.60.

Testing limitation
Offline validation checks code/scene structure and important invariants.
Godot runtime behavior and combat feel were NOT tested here; Godot executable
is unavailable in this environment.
