GHOST GIRL v1.54 — SOFT LANTERN BLOOM / LEFT BOW / GLOW ARROWS / DRESS FILIGREE
===============================================================================
INCREMENTAL PATCH. Requires an assembled and working v1.53 project.

INSTALL
1. Close Godot and back up the project folder + save data.
2. Extract this zip INTO the GhostGirl2 folder containing project.godot.
   The archive starts with scripts/ and assets/ (no extra GhostGirl2 directory).
   Overwrite all 12 matching source/asset files, including the 4 PNG atlases.
3. Reopen Godot. Allow it to reimport the replacement bow atlases and shaders.
4. Play from F5 with your EXISTING SAVE. No reset is needed.

CHANGES
- Weapons: replaces wide straight-edged beams and polygon fan effects with
  feathered colored glow around the same weapon guides. Idle light is softer;
  attacks still pulse. Rare signatures are retained as small, readable effects
  without fan-shaped triangles behind the character.
- Bow: reduces the opaque recolor mask to subtle translucent enamel so the
  original bow shape/string are visible, even with bright gradients.
- Left-facing bow: reorients only the bow's original art/mask in the two left
  locomotion rows (idle left, walk left) for Thornrose and Moonveil. No movement,
  shooting or right/front/back frames were changed. The approved Ghost Girl
  body, hair and dress are not replaced with generated character sprites.
- Arrows: every physical bow arrow now has a separate additive 3-color soft
  lantern glow and a fading motion trail. It follows the parent projectile and
  its saved bow gradient, including charged and normal shots. This cosmetic
  child has no collision, damage, or extra projectiles.
- Dress: stable, low-contrast rose-like embroidered filigree appears on the
  skirt of an equipped dress. Its orientation follows the dress's saved cosmetic
  look, not the weapon. No glowing patterns over hair/eyes.
- Existing 128-bit cosmetic UID, stat rolls, saved favorites and all saves are
  left untouched. No combat or loot changes.

GODOT PLAYTEST PRIORITIES (NOT engine-tested in this environment)
A. Equip sword, bow, scythe, and wand: verify glow fades smoothly at the edge,
   remains visible but doesn't hide the painted weapon.
B. With Thornrose bow and Moonveil bow, idle+walk facing LEFT and then shoot
   LEFT: verify upright non-jumping bow alignment and frame continuity.
C. Shoot normal and charged arrows with differently colored saved bow looks:
   arrow head and wake should glow while target/damage behavior stays the same.
D. Equip a dress without a sword, change/favorite palette, reload: skirt's
   delicate embroidered pattern should remain while hair remains untouched.
E. If any right-facing bow frame became mirrored, that would be a bug: only
   left idle/walking rows should have changed.

LIMITATIONS
Static and image-difference checks have passed but Godot F5 renderer, shader
compilation, bow rotation transition, and real trail visibility could not be
verified without running the Godot engine. Please report engine errors and a
short gameplay screenshot if any offset remains.
