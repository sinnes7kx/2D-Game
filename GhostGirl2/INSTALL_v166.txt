Ghost Girl v1.66 — One-Hand Bow Artwork & Jewel Echoes
Applies directly over assembled v1.65 (not an older patch).

What's new:
- Both Thornrose and Moonveil now draw *actual painted bow-only sprites*
  while using the empty-hand body idle/walk art. Back faces render behind her.
- Left-facing carry mirrors the right-facing bow and the matching glow axes.
- All old shooting/draw/release animations are preserved without modification.
- Malady, Delirium and Exile get three unique 96x96 jewel icons, gold inventory
  borders, and an immediate brief confirmation when socketed.
- Socketed named jewels show their names in compact tooltips. On weapons they
  contribute tiny distinct drifting light motes, visual only. Dresses and
  accessories with named jewels also gain tiny matching fireflies.

Install:
1. Back up your GhostGirl2 project and existing saves.
2. Close Godot.
3. Extract this ZIP inside the GhostGirl2 project folder (project.godot root),
   overwriting matching scripts, scenes/player.tscn, and jewel item definitions.
4. Reopen Godot to import the two bow atlases and jewel icons.
5. Test idle/walk in all four directions, then shoot left; socket a named jewel.

No item stat/loot probability/save schema changes. Jewel drop odds remain as v1.65.
No Godot F5/engine runtime available here, so alignment and feel must be tested.
